About us

Who we are

A community organisation that celebrates and promotes Nepalese creativity and knowledge in Britain.

Our story

A forum for Nepalese arts, science and literature

The Nepalese Arts, Sciences & Literature Forum UK brings together people who love Nepalese culture and creativity, from writers and artists to filmmakers, scientists, engineers, doctors and architects.

We organise literary evenings, art and film events, talks by professionals and cultural programmes. We connect Nepalese professionals and creatives across the UK, and help young people of Nepalese heritage stay connected to their language and culture.

The forum works to celebrate Nepalese arts, science and literature in the UK by hosting events and talk shows.Our aim
  • Talk shows with leading figures from Nepali cinema and the arts
  • Literary evenings, poetry and book launches
  • Talks by Nepalese professionals in science, medicine and engineering
  • Concerts and cultural programmes

What we do

Three pillars, one community

Everything we do brings people together around Nepalese arts, science and literature.

Arts

Visual arts, film, music, dance, architecture and design, showcasing Nepalese creative traditions and new talent in the UK.

Science

Talks and workshops from Nepalese scientists, engineers, doctors and researchers, with mentoring and STEM inspiration for young people.

Literature

Poetry evenings, book launches, storytelling and writing competitions in Nepali and English.
