What we do

Three pillars, one community

Everything we do brings people together around Nepalese arts, science and literature.

Arts

Visual arts, film, music, dance, architecture and design, showcasing Nepalese creative traditions and new talent in the UK.

Science

Talks and workshops from Nepalese scientists, engineers, doctors and researchers, with mentoring and STEM inspiration for young people.

Literature

Poetry evenings, book launches, storytelling and writing competitions in Nepali and English.
